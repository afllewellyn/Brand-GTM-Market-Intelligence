# Historical GTM Plays (Prototype Era)

> **UNVERIFIED HISTORICAL PROTOTYPE OUTPUT.** These three plays came out of
> the original AirOps workflow run against the ElevenLabs example use case.
> The underlying evidence rows were not retained, and none of this should
> be read as current ElevenLabs strategy or a validated market finding.
> They are preserved to show what the prototype produced.

The prototype's Top 3 plays mapped to the three questions an enterprise
buyer must answer before purchasing:

| Buyer question | Play |
|---|---|
| Does it make economic sense? | **1. Enterprise ROI Calculator** |
| Can we safely deploy it? | **2. Enterprise Compliance / Security Framework** |
| Will it perform at production scale? | **3. Enterprise Performance Proof Pack** |

## 1. Enterprise ROI Calculator

Pricing/ROI was the prototype's second-largest theme bucket (75 matches in
that unverified run). The play: an interactive calculator plus a gated
worksheet that lets a Head of CX model cost-per-conversation against their
current contact center economics. Mid-stage asset.

## 2. Enterprise Compliance / Security Framework

Compliance/security matches (24 in that run) concentrated in intent-stage
queries — consistent with procurement and risk review entering the cycle.
The play: a public security overview plus a detailed pack for prospects
under NDA. Mid-stage blocker removal.

## 3. Enterprise Performance Proof Pack

Performance-validation matches (38) skewed toward benchmark and case-study
language. The play: a published benchmark methodology plus reference
stories for late-stage deals. Late-stage win-rate asset.

The logic — ROI, then compliance, then proof — is the reusable part.
The numbers are not.
